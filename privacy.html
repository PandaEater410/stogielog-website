Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StogieLog ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the StogieLog mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

When you create a StogieLog account, we collect information such as your username, email address, and display name. Your password (or other authentication credential) is sent directly to our backend authentication service to create and verify your account. We do not store your password in plain text, and the App does not retain your password after sign-in — only a secure session credential is kept on your device so you can stay signed in. This account information is required to operate your account.

Cigar & Check-In Content

Information you add to the App, such as cigar check-ins, ratings, tasting notes, humidor entries, and favorites, is collected and stored so it can be displayed back to you. Each check-in can be marked private or shared with your friends within the App; StogieLog does not have a public feed that exposes your check-ins to users you are not connected with.

Social Interactions

StogieLog includes optional social features: sending and accepting friend requests, tagging friends on a check-in, commenting on check-ins, and sending direct messages to friends. If you use these features, we collect the related content (such as the comment text or message content) and the information needed to operate them (such as your list of friend connections). Participation in social features is optional, and this content is only visible to you and, where applicable, the friends you are connected with — not to the public.

Support Requests

If you contact us for support, report a bug, or submit feedback, we collect the information you provide in that communication, including your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can respond.

Device & App Information

StogieLog does not use analytics, tracking, or diagnostic-reporting tools, and we do not collect device identifiers, operating system version, or app-usage analytics. Ordinary network communication is required for the App to send and receive your data from our backend, in the same way any networked app communicates with its servers.

Purchase Location Search (Premium)

Premium members can search for the business where they purchased a cigar. When you use this search, the App may ask for your device's location (only while the App is in use, and only if you grant permission) to help find nearby businesses, or you can search by typing a location instead. Your search text and, if available, a one-time location reading are sent to our backend, which queries the Foursquare Places API on your behalf and returns matching businesses to the App. StogieLog does not continuously track your location, does not store a history of your device's location, and only requests your location in the moment you open this search.

Purchase & Subscription Information

If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive and store limited entitlement information necessary to confirm and manage your Premium access (such as whether you have an active subscription or lifetime purchase, and when it expires). We do not receive or store your payment card details, and we do not process payments ourselves — purchases, billing, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple.

Optional Features vs. Required Account Information

Account information (such as your username and email address) is required to create and operate a StogieLog account. Features such as friend connections, commenting, direct messages, sharing check-ins with friends, Premium purchase-location search, and Premium subscriptions are optional, and you can use core journaling features — logging check-ins, ratings, and your humidor — without participating in them.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App's features;
  • Allow you to log check-ins, ratings, and humidor entries, and display them back to you;
  • Enable social features you choose to use, such as friend connections, tagging friends, comments, and direct messages;
  • Perform a purchase-location search when you choose to use that Premium feature;
  • Respond to support requests, bug reports, and feedback;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App, including blocking, reporting, and moderation;
  • Manage Premium entitlement and subscription status; and
  • Improve and troubleshoot the App.

How We Store and Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users, as directed by you: content you choose to share, such as check-ins marked visible to friends, comments, or tagged friends, is visible only to you and the friends you are connected with — not the general public.
  • With our backend service provider: StogieLog uses Supabase to provide authentication, data storage, and backend functionality for the App. Supabase processes your information as necessary to operate these services on our behalf.
  • With Foursquare, for purchase-location search: if you use the Premium purchase-location search feature, your search text and, if available, a one-time location reading are sent through our backend to the Foursquare Places API to return matching businesses. Foursquare does not receive your account identity.
  • With Apple: Premium subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, subject to Apple's own privacy practices.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business transfers: if StogieLog is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Blocking, Reporting, and Moderation

If you block another user, we use that information to prevent interactions between blocked accounts within the App. If you report content or another user, we collect the details of your report — such as the reason, the content involved, and any notes you provide — to review and take appropriate action. We also keep a record of your own submitted reports so you can track them.

Where a report or other information indicates a violation of our Terms of Use, authorized reviewers may take moderation action, which can include removing content or restricting or banning an account. If your account is restricted or banned, we retain the information needed to explain the restriction to you and to support an appeal. You may submit an appeal of a moderation decision directly within the App; we use the information you provide in your appeal to review the decision.

We may retain reports, moderation decisions, and related security information beyond normal account deletion where reasonably necessary to investigate violations, prevent abuse, and protect the safety of our community and service.

Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may update certain account information directly within the App. You can also request deletion of your account directly within the App, or by contacting us at privacy@stogielog.app. Once you request deletion, your account and associated profile and social data are scheduled for removal by our backend. This action cannot be undone once it has been completed.

We may retain certain information after deletion where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or to enforce our agreements — for example, records related to a moderation action, report, or account ban may be retained in anonymized or limited form for these purposes.

Age Requirements

StogieLog relates to cigar enjoyment and is not directed to children. The App is not intended for use by individuals who are not of legal age to purchase tobacco products in their jurisdiction.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at privacy@stogielog.app.
