Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Insert Effective Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how StogieLog ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the StogieLog mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

When you create a StogieLog account, we collect information such as your username, email address, and password (stored securely). This information is required to operate your account.

Cigar & Check-In Content

Information you add to the App, such as cigar check-ins, ratings, notes, humidor entries, and favorites, is collected and stored so it can be displayed back to you and, where you choose to share it, to your friends within the App.

Social Interactions

If you use social features, we collect information related to those interactions, such as the friends you follow, comments, likes, and content you share with other users. Participation in social features is optional.

Support Requests

If you contact us for support, report a bug, or submit feedback, we collect the information you provide in that communication, including your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can respond.

Device & App Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for the App to function correctly, such as device type, operating system version, and app version. This information is used for compatibility and troubleshooting purposes.

Purchase & Subscription Information

If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive limited information necessary to confirm and manage your subscription status (such as whether you have an active Premium subscription). We do not receive or store your full payment card details, which are handled entirely by Apple.

Optional Features vs. Required Account Information

Account information (such as your username and email address) is required to create and operate a StogieLog account. Features such as social connections, sharing check-ins publicly, and Premium subscriptions are optional, and you can use core journaling features without participating in them.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App's features;
  • Allow you to log check-ins, ratings, and humidor entries, and display them back to you;
  • Enable social features you choose to use, such as following friends;
  • Respond to support requests, bug reports, and feedback;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App;
  • Manage Premium subscription status; and
  • Improve and troubleshoot the App.

How We Store and Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users, as directed by you: content you choose to share through social features (such as check-ins or comments) may be visible to your friends or, depending on your settings, other users of the App.
  • With service providers: we may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or backend services) to operate the App. These providers are only permitted to use your information as necessary to provide services to us.
  • With Apple: Premium subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, subject to Apple's own privacy practices.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business transfers: if StogieLog is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Blocking, Reporting, and Moderation

If you block another user, we use that information to prevent interactions between blocked accounts within the App. If you report content or another user, we collect the details of your report, along with relevant context, to review and take appropriate moderation action. Reports may be used to enforce our Terms of Use and to protect the safety of our community.

Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may update certain account information directly within the App. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us at privacy@stogielog.app. We will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize your information, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes (for example, records needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements).

Age Requirements

StogieLog relates to cigar enjoyment and is not directed to children. The App is not intended for use by individuals who are not of legal age to purchase tobacco products in their jurisdiction.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at privacy@stogielog.app.
