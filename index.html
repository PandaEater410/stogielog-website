Your Cigar Journey, Beautifully Organized

StogieLog is a social cigar journal and rating app for iOS. Discover new cigars, log every check-in, rate what you smoke, and keep your humidor organized — all while connecting with friends who share your passion.

What You Can Do With StogieLog

Discover & Search Cigars

Browse and search a growing catalog of cigars to find your next smoke.

Check In Your Smokes

Log each cigar you smoke, building a personal history over time.

Rate & Review

Rate cigars and keep notes on what you liked — or didn't — for next time.

Save Favorites

Bookmark the cigars you love so you can easily find them again.

Manage Your Humidor

Keep track of the cigars in your collection, right from your phone.

Connect With Friends

Follow friends, see their check-ins, and share your cigar journey together.

Free to Start, Premium When You're Ready

StogieLog is free to use. An optional Premium subscription is available for members who want to unlock additional features. You can enjoy the core journaling and social experience without ever subscribing.
